A very simple PDF reader. It opens a PDF and gets out of the way.

• The pages fill the screen, one under the other, with nothing on the edges. A tap shows a small bar with the page number and Share; another tap hides it.
• Pinch or double tap to zoom, drag and fling to move. A zoomed page is drawn again sharp.
• Long press a word to select it, keep the finger down to select more, then Copy. Whole page selects the whole page.
• Pages that are pictures (scans) get their text read on the phone when you long press them, only then and only that page.
• Share: Add to Dropbox when the Dropbox app is installed, the two apps you share PDFs to most, and the system share sheet.
• Set it as your default PDF viewer: open a PDF, pick Opensolr PDF Reader, tap Always.

No network permission. No account. No ads. No tracking. Your PDFs stay on your phone unless you share them.
